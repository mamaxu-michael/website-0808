About

Founded back in 2000, we are an award-winning supplier of blank garments, ready for decoration. Built with a strong ethical and environmental ethos, we were the first people in the printwear industry to introduce organic cotton to the market in 2005. We furthered our commitment to sustainability in 2019, when we successfully switched out all the cotton in our production to organic.

We aim to change the industry’s perceptions by educating & collaborating with our suppliers, team, customers, competitors, non-profits and other stakeholders to make meaningful change.

We offer ranges suitable for adults, kids and babies, and also have a bespoke team that specialise in producing clothing for brands and companies looking for something specific yet sustainable.

Sustainability

Company Practices

Energy Efficiency

Pledge to phase out sources of coal-fired heat and power generation in our own companies and direct suppliers from 2025.
Wash Care instructions to wash at low temperatures.

Water Efficiency

Organic farming uses less water as the cotton is more likely to be rain fed as is our African cotton.
Our Factories all have a very high first-time-right dying ratio.
Water from ETPs is reused for plumbing and cleaning of machinery.

Chemical Management

Factories are GOTS® certified and are regularly checked by the regulatory bodies. In addition we make personal visits to factories and dyeing mills to make sure the chemicals are stored and handled safely.

Pollution Reduction

Our Factories all have functioning and adequate Effluent Treatment Plants.
To avoid air pollution we use mostly sea freight for our shipments, and are continuously reducing the amount of plastic packing.
Office is powered by renewable energy.

Recycling & Waste

Using rPET from consumer waste in more styles year on year.
No garments go to landfill, if not sold, they are donated to charity or to textile recycling.
Any non-recyclable office waste is sent to Energy Recovery Facilities.

Organic Materials

We’re transitioning out of conventional cotton to fully organic across the whole range – certified to GOTS and OCS Standards. Currently 60% of our range is organic and we target 100% by 2021. We work with a GOTS certified organic supply chain in Tanzania

Environmentally Friendly Materials (Excluding Organic)

We are committed to only using sustainable fibres by 2021. We collaborate with Canopy for traceable viscose and introduced recycled polyester. We partner with innovative suppliers to develop fibre sources that reduce environmental and social impacts.

Animal Friendly

We do not use animal products. We are PETA Vegan certified.

Sustainable Business Model

‘Well Made’ means high quality long-life garments with responsible production and sustainable fibres. We recognise the lifecycle of every garment we make and that each part of its journey can benefit everyone involved with minimal environmental impact.

Fair Trade

We work with closely with our factories for fair pricing ensuring workers are supported, paid a decent wage and are ensured safe and fair working conditions. Organic cotton farmers are paid a premium.

Decent Working Conditions

Work with BSCI compliant factories, ensuring social standards are met. We make personal visits and often speak with workers directly. We encourage improvement for the workplace at each factory depending on their needs and location.

Ethical Sourcing & Supply Chain Management

We only work with trusted, certified suppliers we know personally, whom we have long term relationships with. We give them adequate time for production, relieving pressure. Require transaction certificates ensuring transparency in the supply chain.

Awards

Sustainability Objectives

  1. We pledge that every single garment in our range will be made exclusively from sustainable fibres – including organic cotton – by 2021.
  2. Fashion Industry Charter for Climate Action: with the other signatories we’ve set an initial target to reduce our aggregate greenhouse gas emissions and phase out sources of coal-fired heat and power.
  3. Our mission is to educate and change the industry perceptions to embrace sustainability in their businesses – longer life cycles, well made.
